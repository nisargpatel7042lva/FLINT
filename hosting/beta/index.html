Flint · Beta

Your runs light up the city.

Run with your club and every block you run turns your club's colour. Stop running and another club takes it. This is the first version, for the clubs testing it with us.

Download for Android

Flint 0.1.0 · Android 8 or newer

Installing it

  1. Tap Download, then open the file.It is in your notifications or your Downloads folder.
  2. Allow installs from your browser.Android asks once, because the beta is not on the Play Store yet. Tap Settings, switch on "Allow from this source", then go back.
  3. Tap Install.If Play Protect warns about an unknown app, tap "More details" then "Install anyway". It warns about every app that is not from the Play Store.
  4. Open Flint and join your club.Your organiser has a 6-letter club code. Enter it after you sign up.

Before your first run

Let it run with the screen off. Flint records with your phone locked, but some phones (Xiaomi, Redmi, Oppo, Vivo, Realme, OnePlus) stop apps to save battery. In Flint's run screen, tap Open battery settings and set Flint to Don't optimise or No restrictions.

Something not right?

In the app, go to You → Report a problem. It reaches the people building Flint directly, with your phone model attached so we can fix it.
